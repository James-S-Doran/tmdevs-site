Roomhub

Invite-only room booking for a small therapy clinic — built for the practitioners who book rooms, and the reception kiosk that shouldn't see why.

The problem

An acupuncture and talking-therapy clinic shares a handful of treatment rooms between several practitioners and a walk-in reception desk. Spreadsheets and group chats led to double-bookings, and client details were visible to anyone who could see the diary — a real problem for a therapy practice.

What I built

  • A practitioner calendar with automatic conflict prevention — two people simply can't book the same room at the same time.
  • A dedicated walk-in kiosk mode for reception: it shows rooms as booked or free, and nothing else — no names, no notes, no reasons.
  • An approval workflow for cancellations and reschedules, with a full audit trail of every change.
  • An admin suite for managing rooms, staff accounts and approvals in one place.
  • Data export and erasure tools built in from day one, not bolted on later, given the sensitivity of therapy records.

Where it stands

Live on TestFlight and in daily use by real clinic staff and reception.
