Dumped

A faster, kinder way to clear your camera roll. Swipe right to keep, left to delete — nothing is actually removed until you confirm the queue.

The problem

Most people are carrying thousands of photos they'll never look at again, and no real way to deal with it. Reviewing a camera roll one photo at a time is tedious, and deleting feels risky — it's easy to second-guess yourself and stop halfway through.

What I built

  • A swipeable deck — keep or delete each photo or video with a flick, with multi-level undo if you change your mind.
  • A safety net: swiped-left photos queue in a "to delete" trash and are only removed from the device once you confirm.
  • Smart filters that do the sorting for you — Live Photos, large files, screenshots, blurred shots, and near-duplicates, all detected on-device.
  • A "Vacation" filter that automatically clusters geotagged photos into trips, away from home, and names them by place.
  • A grid view with pinch-to-select for batch decisions, plus milestone badges to mark progress without turning it into a competition.

Where it stands

Live on the App Store. Free to use, with a one-time lifetime unlock for unlimited daily deletes — no subscription, no ads.
