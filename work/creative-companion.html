Creative Companion

A private, calm archive of a creative life — for actors, writers, musicians and artists to notice the progress they'd otherwise overlook.

The problem

Progress in a creative practice is slow, quiet, and easy to forget you've made. Most self-improvement apps answer that with streaks, leaderboards and engagement-bait notifications — exactly the kind of pressure that makes creative work feel worse, not better.

What I built

  • A private timeline for reflections, captured as text, photo, audio or video.
  • "On this day" resurfacing — a quiet reminder of where you were a month, or a year, ago.
  • A gentle progress review, built around noticing rather than scoring.
  • Mentor share: a private, expiring, read-only link so a coach or teacher can see progress without needing an account of their own.
  • Keepsake exports — a whole timeline laid out as a beautiful PDF to keep.

The one rule that shaped every decision: no streaks, no follower counts, no social comparison, no infinite scroll. If a feature request pulled toward any of those, it didn't ship.

Where it stands

In a private trial with a small group of creative friends, gathering real feedback before any decisions are made about pricing or new features.
