Doctor's Note

The same calm-logging philosophy as Creative Companion, rebuilt from the ground up for medical students and junior doctors.

The problem

A friend training as a doctor saw Creative Companion and asked for the same thing for medicine: somewhere to track clinical skills, study sessions and small wins through training — without it turning into a gradebook or a competition with other students.

What I built

  • One flexible log covering reflections, skills, study sessions and milestones, rather than four separate tools.
  • A confidence rating framed as "how did this feel", not a grade — so it captures growth honestly rather than performing for a score.
  • A progress view that groups entries by topic and surfaces what's genuinely improving, and what's quietly falling behind, based on the trend in your own ratings over time.
  • Free-form tags rather than a rigid competency framework, so it flexes from first-year student to junior doctor without forcing a fit.

Where it stands

MVP built on the same proven architecture as Creative Companion, backend live, and early testing with real medical students is just getting under way.
